What's This All About?

Everyone's so pumped to build new stuff, but who's talking about fixing what we already have? Tech moves fast, but sometimes we need to slow down and look around.

Low Orbit Club is part meetup, part social hour, part systems thinking hangout. No panels, no pitching, no networking theater. Just interesting people talking about how to make tomorrow (literally tomorrow, not some far-off future) a little better than today.
