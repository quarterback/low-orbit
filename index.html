What is Low Orbit Club?

After decades of "move fast and break things," we're gathering to slow down, reflect, and repair. Low Orbit Club is a convening space for people working in tech who want to talk about what's broken and imagine ways to fix it.

Not a meetup, not a conference, not a networking event—this is a space for honest conversation about the systems we build, maintain, and inhabit. We focus on the speculative present: what could be different by tomorrow, not some distant future.

Core Principles

Repair Over Disruption

We believe in fixing broken systems rather than constantly replacing them. Maintenance is a form of innovation.

Reflection Over Speed

We create space to pause, consider consequences, and act with intention rather than racing to the next shiny thing.

Connection Over Isolation

We bring people together across disciplines and roles to share perspectives and find common ground in an atomized world.
