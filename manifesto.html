The Low Orbit Manifesto

For the speculative present and the practice of repair

Most of us are trying to make things work from inside the mess. Not above it, not after it—just… here. Right now.

We're not starting a movement. We're just creating room. Room to notice what's broken, name what's misaligned, and consider what we might do with what we've got.

Low Orbit Club is for people who feel the tension between how things work and how they should work. People who work in systems, around systems, or despite them. People who carry a quiet sense that maybe things don't have to be this way.

Why "Low Orbit"?

Because we're staying close to earth. Close to lived experience. Not lost in the clouds of hype or theory. Not rocketing to futures we can't yet touch. Low orbit is where observation meets proximity. It's where we work, where we watch, and where we try to repair.

What We Believe

This isn't about purity or performance. It's about practice. We believe in care, critique, and collective maintenance. We believe in the dignity of debugging systems no one understands. We believe in repair as a form of resistance.

What You Can Expect

No panels. No pitches. No performance reviews disguised as conversation. Just some chairs, some prompts, maybe some snacks. And a group of people curious about the mechanics of the present and what's still possible within it.

This is a space to:

  • Talk honestly about what's broken
  • Share tools, strategies, or just questions
  • Test ideas that don't fit elsewhere
  • Remember that others are trying too
