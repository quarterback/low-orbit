The Low Orbit Manifesto

A declaration for the speculative present and the practice of repair

Most meetups are broken. Most conferences are performances. Most networking events are transactional.

After living through over two decades of people moving fast and breaking things, optimizing for speed and scale, chasing KPIs and constantly ramping up, we need spaces to slow down, reflect, and repair.

Low Orbit Club is a convening around repair, careful iteration, and a refusal to accept that our only fate is to be subsumed by machines.

Why Low Orbit?

We operate close to the ground—in the messy reality of systems as they exist today. Not in the stratosphere of futurism or the vacuum of theory, but in the low orbit where real work happens and real change is possible.

The Speculative Present

While everyone is obsessed with speculative futures, we're focused on the speculative present—what could be different by tomorrow, not in some distant future. What small changes could create ripples? What constraints are we accepting that could be questioned? What repairs are possible right now?

A Different Kind of Gathering

We believe in creating spaces for dialogue that matters. No keynotes, no pitches, no performative networking. Just honest conversations about the systems we build, maintain, and inhabit.

We gather to:

  • Share experiences of what's broken in the systems we work within
  • Question the defaults and assumptions that shape our work
  • Imagine alternative approaches beyond the dominant narratives
  • Find solidarity in the practice of repair and maintenance
  • Build connections based on shared values, not just shared job titles

An Invitation

This is an invitation to join us in creating something different. Not another professional association or networking group, but a space for the conversations we need to be having.

If you're tired of moving fast and breaking things, if you believe in the value of repair, if you want to imagine alternatives to the status quo—Low Orbit Club is for you.
